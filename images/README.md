# Work photos

Put photos of finished jobs here so they appear in the **Our Work** section of the website.

- Name them `work-1.jpg`, `work-2.jpg` … up to `work-8.jpg` (lower-case, `.jpg`).
- `work-1.jpg` is shown largest, so use your best photo for it.
- Landscape photos look best. Resize them to about 1600 px wide (under ~400 KB each) so the page stays fast on mobile data.
- To change a caption, edit the matching `<figcaption>` in `index.html` (search for `work-1.jpg`).

Missing photos are hidden automatically, and the whole section stays hidden until at least one photo is added.

## Main picture at the top of the page

The top of the page shows **`hero.jpg`**: an AI-generated picture (made with Gemini) of a yellow wall with a hand-painted Tamil message.
To change it, replace `hero.jpg` with another landscape picture, about 1024 × 576 px or larger. If `hero.jpg` is missing, the page falls back to the drawing in `hero-art.svg`.
`og-image.png` (the WhatsApp/Facebook preview) also contains this picture and is made separately, so ask whoever edits the site to remake it when the picture changes.

A real photo of your own team on a job is best. To make one with an AI image tool (ChatGPT, Gemini, Microsoft Designer and similar), you can use this prompt:

> A real documentary photograph, not an illustration or cartoon. A Tamil man in his 40s, a traditional wall sign painter,
> wearing a faded checked shirt and a folded lungi, barefoot, standing on scaffolding made of casuarina poles tied with coir rope,
> hand-painting a large advertisement on the plastered side wall of a two-storey building on a street in Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu.
> The advertisement is half finished: big flat areas of orange, yellow and navy blue paint, with the unpainted part still showing
> a chalk outline. There is no text, letters or logo anywhere in the image; the lettering area is left as plain painted blocks.
> Small enamel paint tins and brushes on the plank. In the background: palmyra palm trees, a tiled-roof house, a parked two-wheeler,
> dusty road. Harsh warm afternoon sunlight, natural colours, shot on a DSLR with a 35 mm lens, eye level, wide landscape format, 4:3.

Check the result before you use it: AI images sometimes get hands or tools wrong, or slip in made-up words.
